About the shop

Two decades of craftsmanship at Hilltop.

Pete and Debbie Nuñez opened Nuñez Fine Jewelers in 2005. Twenty years on, it is still a family-owned fixture at Hilltop in Virginia Beach, with more than forty years of design and restoration experience behind the bench.

Because the work is done on-site, you deal directly with the people doing it, repairs come back quickly, and your jewelry never leaves the shop unnecessarily. Honest advice and quality work, every time.
